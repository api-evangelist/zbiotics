Key Takeaways Heat and alcohol both trigger vasodilation, so drinking on a hot day adds more strain to your body's cooling system. Even mild dehydration from the heat can change how alcohol affects you, increasing your level of impairment more than you expect or realize, and making you more likely to take risks.
